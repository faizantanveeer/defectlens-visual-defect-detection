These example images are taken from the held-out test split of the **MVTec Anomaly Detection
(MVTec AD)** dataset, category `metal_nut`, unmodified.

* Paul Bergmann, Michael Fauser, David Sattlegger, Carsten Steger. *MVTec AD — A Comprehensive
  Real-World Dataset for Unsupervised Anomaly Detection.* CVPR 2019.
